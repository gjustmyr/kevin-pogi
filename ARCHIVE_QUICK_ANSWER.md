# Will I Still See Deans and Faculty After Archiving?

## YES! ✅

**Deans and Faculty are ALWAYS visible, regardless of academic year status.**

## What's NOT Affected by Archive

```
✅ ALWAYS VISIBLE:
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ • Deans                             │
│ • Faculty                           │
│ • Organizations                     │
│ • User Accounts                     │
│ • Personal Profiles                 │
│ • PDS (Personal Data Sheet)         │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## What IS Affected by Archive

```
📦 FILTERED BY ACADEMIC YEAR:
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ • Faculty Requirements              │
│ • Faculty Clearance Records         │
│ • Organization Documents            │
│ • Organization Members              │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Example

### You Archive: 2023-2024

**Still See:**
- ✅ Dean John Doe
- ✅ Faculty Jane Smith
- ✅ Engineering Society Organization

**Archived (Hidden in Active View):**
- 📦 Jane Smith's requirements for 2023-2024
- 📦 Jane Smith's clearance for 2023-2024
- 📦 Engineering Society documents for 2023-2024
- 📦 Engineering Society members for 2023-2024

## Why?

**Deans and Faculty tables DO NOT have `academic_year_id` column.**

They exist independently of academic years.

## Bottom Line

Archive academic years safely! Your deans and faculty will always be visible and accessible. Only year-specific documents and records are filtered by academic year.
