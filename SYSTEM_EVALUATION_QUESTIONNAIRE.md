## SCORING GUIDE

**Total Possible Score:** 200 points (40 questions × 5 points each)

**Rating Scale:**
- **180-200 points (90-100%):** Excellent - System exceeds quality standards
- **160-179 points (80-89%):** Very Good - System meets quality standards with minor improvements needed
- **140-159 points (70-79%):** Good - System is functional but needs several improvements
- **120-139 points (60-69%):** Fair - System needs significant improvements
- **Below 120 points (<60%):** Poor - System requires major revisions
