# Faculty Notifications - Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 Quick Access

**For Deans:**
1. Login to your dean account
2. Click **"Faculty Notifications"** in the left sidebar
3. Start sending notifications!

---

## 📧 Send a Notification in 4 Steps

### Step 1: Select Recipients
- ✅ Browse your department's faculty list
- ✅ Use search to find specific faculty
- ✅ Check boxes next to faculty names
- ✅ Or click "Select All" to choose everyone

### Step 2: Choose a Template (Optional)
Click one of the quick templates:
- 📋 **Reminder** - For deadline reminders
- 📅 **Meeting** - For meeting invitations
- 📢 **Announcement** - For general announcements

### Step 3: Compose Your Message
- Enter a **Subject** (required)
- Write your **Message** (required)
- Edit the template or write from scratch

### Step 4: Send
- Click **"Send Notification"**
- Confirm in the dialog
- Done! ✅

---

## 💡 Tips

### Search Faculty
Type in the search box to filter by:
- Name
- Employee ID
- Email address

### Character Limits
- Subject: 200 characters max
- Message: 2000 characters max

### Select Multiple Faculty
- Check individual boxes
- Or use "Select All" for everyone
- Click "Clear Selection" to start over

### Message Formatting
- Line breaks are preserved
- Keep it professional
- Be clear and concise

Faculty members will receive a professional email with:

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "No faculty members found"
- Check if faculty are registered in your department
- Try clearing the search filter

### "Please select at least one faculty member"
- You must select recipients before sending
- Check at least one faculty member

### "Subject is required"
- Enter a subject line before sending

### "Message is required"
- Enter a message before sending

### Email not received?
- Check faculty email addresses are correct
- Check spam/junk folders
- Verify SMTP settings (contact admin)
